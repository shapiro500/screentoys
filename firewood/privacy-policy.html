Privacy Policy

Firewood Splitting Simulator does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data. The app has no ads, analytics, tracking, user accounts, or developer-operated servers. Game settings and progress are stored locally on your device.

The app uses Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements and Apple StoreKit for its optional in-app purchase. Apple may process Game Center activity and purchase information according to Apple’s Privacy Policy. I do not receive or store your payment information.

Because I do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal data for me to retain or delete.
